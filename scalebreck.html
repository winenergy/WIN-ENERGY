Visual Evidence

Severe Limescale Encrustation Inside Plumbing & Showers

Without treatment, dissolved calcium carbonate crystallizes into rock-hard calcite scale. SCALEBRECK alters ion structures to aragonite crystal habits, preventing surface bonding and gently dissolving preexisting encrustations over continuous water flow.

Engineering Principle

Working Principle & 4-Stage Mechanism

SCALEBRECK applies an advanced hydro-polarization electronic method directly onto your existing pipeline without chemical dosing:

Stage 01

Signal Generation

The control unit transmits a complex, modulated signal from an internal converter running at a low, non-hazardous potential of ±12 V DC.

Stage 02

Induction Field

A dedicated signal cable wrapped externally around the water supply pipe delivers an alternating square-wave frequency ranging between 1,000 to 20,000 cycles/sec (1 kHz – 20 kHz).

Stage 03

Ionic Alteration

The induced variable electrical field disrupts and modifies the physical structure of hard mineral ions (calcium/magnesium), altering their crystal habits and boosting overall solubility.

Stage 04

Descaling Action

Conditioned water retains the capacity to absorb more dissolved minerals, preventing new deposit formation while gradually dissolving existing limescale within downstream plumbing.

Compliance & Transparency

Official Disclaimers & Operational Boundaries

To maintain rigorous technical integrity and customer transparency:

Not a Water Purifier / Softener

ScaleBreak is strictly an electronic water conditioner. It is not a conventional salt-based water softener, drinking-water purifier, RO system, disinfectant, or sterilizer.

Dissolved Minerals & TDS

The device does not chemically remove calcium or magnesium ions from water, nor does it eliminate Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) or make non-potable water potable. Beneficial minerals remain in the water.

No Substitute for Filtration / Biosecurity

It does not replace mechanical sand/disc filtration, laboratory water testing, approved sanitation (chlorination/disinfection), or standard pipeline flush routines.

Not an Agrochemical Substitute

In farming, ScaleBreak is not a fertilizer, pesticide, or plant growth regulator. It works alongside proper agronomic practices, crop nutrition, and irrigation scheduling.

Performance Variability: Real-world scale management depends upon source water chemistry, flow rates, pipeline materials, environmental temperature, and correct installation. Numerical agricultural benefits should only be cited when verified by company-approved field trials.
