Water Sector 03

Industrial Boilers, Cooling Towers & Plants

Industrial High-Capacity Multi-Coil

Industrial water loops, cooling towers, and boiler networks are highly vulnerable to mineral crystallization. A 1mm scale layer on boiler plates or heat exchangers increases fuel consumption by up to 10–15% due to its severe thermal insulation effect.

  • Thermal Insulation Elimination: Prevents hard calcite accumulation, sustaining optimal thermal conductivity in heat exchangers.
  • No Toxic Acid Cleaning: Eliminates hazardous descaling acids and recurring chemical softeners, creating clean discharge water.
  • Boiler Blowdown Reduction: Reduces blowdown cycles and conserves millions of liters of treated industrial water.

Water Sector 04

Drip Irrigation & Agricultural Borewells

Zero Drip Emitter Clogs

Hard borewell water crystallizes into calcite, blocking tiny drip irrigation emitters and micro-sprinklers. Over time, salt crusts form on topsoil, locking out essential fertilizers and stunting root development.

  • Unclogged Drip Lines: Converts calcite into aragonite, allowing water to flow evenly across every plant row.
  • Breaks Hard Soil Crusts: Improves soil porosity, enabling deep water infiltration and enhanced root fertilizer uptake.
  • Protects Borewell Pumps: Prevents mineral scaling on submersible pump impellers, extending pump lifetime.
