The Science Behind HydroPulse Technology

Smarter Water Conditioning. Without Added Chemicals.

Hard water contains natural minerals like calcium and magnesium. When these minerals build up over time, they form limescale on pipes, heating elements, and other equipment. This buildup can affect performance, increase maintenance needs, and reduce heat-transfer efficiency.

HydroPulse uses electronic signals to influence how these minerals behave in water. The minerals remain in the water, but their tendency to form stubborn scale deposits can be reduced — without adding chemicals or salt.

01

1. Limescale Control

Helps prevent new scale and loosen existing deposits.

HydroPulse helps reduce new limescale buildup while helping break down and detach scale that has already accumulated on pipes and equipment.

02

2. Non-Contact Technology

No added chemicals, salt, or filters.

HydroPulse uses electronic signals to condition water without adding chemicals or salt. The natural minerals remain in the water rather than being removed through conventional softening.

03

3. Biofilm and Algae Control

Helps manage unwanted biological buildup.

By helping control limescale deposits and the surfaces where buildup can occur, HydroPulse can support efforts to manage biofilm and algae inside the applicable water systems.

04

4. Simple, Non-Intrusive Installation

Install it. Switch it on. Let it work.

The HydroPulse unit can be clamped onto the outside of the main pipeline, without requiring the device to sit inside the water flow. Once installed and powered, it can operate without continuous manual intervention.

Solar Agri-Tech Physics

Nature Attracts Them. Intelligence Takes Over.

A smarter way to protect your crops - powered by sunlight, guided by intelligent technology, and designed to work with minimal human intervention.

BuzzZap harnesses a natural insect behaviour called positive phototaxis - the tendency of many flying insects to move towards light. Using carefully selected light wavelengths, an intelligent microcontroller, and an automated operating cycle, it helps draw target crop enemy insects towards the device for controlled elimination.

Powered by solar energy, BuzzZap brings together smart sensing, automatic timing, and protective circuitry in one simple, field-ready solution. It is designed to do the work on its own, so you can spend less time managing pest-control equipment and more time focusing on your crops.

The intelligence behind the simplicity

01

1. Light That Lures

Targeted light attraction

Many nocturnal crop pest insects are naturally attracted to particular wavelengths of light. BuzzZap uses a carefully selected light spectrum to attract susceptible flying insects towards the device, helping bring pest control closer to where it is needed.

02

2. Intelligence That Knows When to Act

Microcontroller-based automation

BuzzZap is designed to operate during programmed pest-activity periods, with automatic timing that reduces unnecessary operation. By concentrating activity around selected nighttime hours, it aims to make pest control more efficient while helping limit exposure during other periods.

03

3. Built to Respond to the Weather

Rain-responsive protection

An integrated moisture sensor detects wet conditions and triggers the designed protective response, suspending high-voltage operation when required. This added layer of protection helps safeguard the system in changing outdoor conditions.

04

4. Powered by the Sun

Autonomous solar operation

A solar panel and integrated charge-management system power BuzzZap without the need for a conventional mains connection. Built-in battery protection helps manage charging and discharge, supporting dependable operation across changing conditions.
