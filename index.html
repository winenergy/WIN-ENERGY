Water Systems Challenge

Limescale Deposition & Flow Inefficiency

Hard water minerals (calcium and magnesium) crystallize into rock-like calcite on heat exchangers, boilers, domestic plumbing, and drip irrigation emitters, choking flow and causing severe energy losses.

Up to 15% energy loss in boilers and cooling systems per mm of scale
Choked drip emitters and damaged hot water geysers
Hazardous acid washes and expensive salt softener maintenance
