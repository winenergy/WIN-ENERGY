Scientific Methodology

Our Approach: Understanding Insect Biology

CropSafe's engineering revolves around studying target crop-specific insect dynamics — distinguishing between friend and enemy insects, mapping peak attack timings, and calibrating exact optical attraction wavelengths:

Phototropic Light Calibration

Nocturnal crop pests possess compound eyes sensitive to specific UV and visible light bandwidths. CropSafe utilizes specialized light frequencies calibrated through rigorous agricultural field trials to maximize pest draw.

Smart Enemy-Window Timing

Enemy insects (borers, moths, beetles) are predominantly active for the first 3 to 4 hours post-sunset. CropSafe turns ON automatically at dusk and shuts OFF after its cycle, ensuring daytime beneficial pollinators (bees, ladybugs) are never harmed.

Non-Stick Physical Grid

Insects drawn to the light cross a calibrated high-voltage electrified wire mesh that neutralizes them instantly. The specialized grid prevents dead insects from adhering, remaining 100% clean for continuous capture.

Continuous Multi-Acre Field Protection

Pole-mounted CropSafe installed directly between crops, operating autonomously night after night with zero fuel, zero grid electricity, and zero chemical handling.

High-Voltage Pest Neutralization Grid

Captures pests before they can lay thousands of eggs in crops. One female moth trapped prevents hundreds of destructive caterpillar larvae in subsequent generations.

Broad Agricultural Spectrum

Targets Over 1,800 Phototactic Flying Pests

Effective across diverse horticulture, cash crops, cereal grains, and plantation estates:

Vineyards & Orchards

Fruit borers, leaf rollers, thrips, stem borers, and moths affecting grapes, pomegranates, mangoes, citrus, and guavas. Prevents fruit blemish and drop.

Vegetables & Polyhouses

Diamondback moths, fruit & shoot borers, whiteflies, and armyworms in tomatoes, brinjal, okra, chili, capsicum, and cabbage.

Cotton & Field Crops

Pink bollworm, American bollworm, spotted bollworm, stem borer, leaf folder in cotton, rice (paddy), sugarcane, and maize.

Plantations & Estates

Tea loopers, red slug caterpillars, coffee white stem borers, and nocturnal beetles in tea, coffee, cardamom, and rubber estates.

Engineering & Intelligence

Advanced Solar Power & Intelligent Sensors

Built for reliable monsoon and winter operations across remote agricultural fields:

Diffuse Light Solar PV

High-efficiency photovoltaic solar panel charges the battery even under cloudy monsoon or foggy conditions, guaranteeing night readiness.

Automatic Rain Sensor

Integrated rain sensor automatically deactivates high-voltage grid during rainfall to prevent short circuits and battery drain, restoring automatically once dry.

Lithium-Ion Battery Safety

Onboard microcontroller manages constant charge current, overcharge protection, deep-discharge cut-off, and short-circuit safety.
