About Win Energy

An innovation-led clean-tech engineering enterprise committed to solving real-world water and agricultural resource challenges through sustainable, maintenance-free technologies.

The Corporate Umbrella

Who We Are: Win Energy

Win Energy represents the unification of cutting-edge physical engineering across water purification systems and precision agricultural technology. Housing our specialized divisions — Mizusun Pvt. Ltd. (creators of HydroPulse Hydro-Polarization Technology) and Leanway Energy Pvt. Ltd. (creators of BuzzZap Clean Solar Agricultural Traps) — Win Energy delivers chemical-free, sustainable alternatives to outdated, high-maintenance practices.

With engineering headquarters in Pune and high-volume manufacturing facilities in Sangli, Maharashtra, our devices are deployed across thousands of farmlands, corporate complexes, and industrial sites throughout India, and exported to international partners across Europe and Africa.

Water Conditioning Wing

Mizusun Pvt. Ltd. (HydroPulse)

Dedicated to developing smart physical water conditioning technology that alters mineral crystallization without salt, chemicals, or filters. Protecting plumbing networks, HVAC chillers, and domestic appliances while ensuring pure, healthy mineral-rich water.

Clean Agriculture Wing

Leanway Energy Pvt. Ltd. (BuzzZap)

Focused on revolutionizing precision agriculture through renewable solar energy, engineering, and phototropic pest attraction. Leading the movement to "Double the Farmer's Income" through pesticide-free, residue-free farming.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop smart, sustainable technologies that conserve resources and address real-world energy, water, and agricultural challenges. We strive to make responsible, chemical-free operations accessible by providing reliable solutions that improve yield, cut waste, and protect future generations.

Our Vision

Our vision is to stand as a global benchmark in sustainable clean engineering — empowering farmers to produce organic, chemical-free harvest while freeing domestic, commercial, and industrial pipelines from limescale scaling and toxic effluent.
