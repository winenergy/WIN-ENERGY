Heavy Industrial Challenges

Thermal Penalty Losses in Industrial Water Loops

Boilers and heat exchangers face compounding thermal resistance from hard mineral deposits.

10–15% Fuel Loss

Just 1mm of hard calcite on boiler tubes creates an insulating barrier that burns an extra 10–15% in gas, diesel, or coal fuel.

Flow Constriction

Thick limescale rings choke pipeline cross-sectional area, driving up pumping head pressure, pump power consumption, and mechanical wear.

Cooling Tower Fouling

Limescale on fill packs traps airborne dirt and algae, creating stubborn biofouling that forces frequent and expensive plant maintenance shutdowns.

Acid Wash Shutdowns

Corrosive acid descaling treatments cause rapid metallurgic pitting in copper and stainless tubes, creating leaks and costly unscheduled downtime.

Engineering Solution

Modulated Capacitive Resonance for Heavy-Duty Plants

Designed for 24/7 manufacturing plants, power generation facilities, chemical processing loops, and sugar mills. HydroPulse’s industrial multi-coil transmitters pulse dynamic capacitive frequencies up to 40,000 Hz through heavy pipe walls without cutting pipelines.

Prevents Thermal Fuel Loss

Keeps heat exchange surfaces 100% free of insulating calcite scale, securing factory design thermal efficiency year-round.

Eliminates Toxic Acid Washes

Zero requirement for hazardous hydrochloric or sulfamic acid flushes. Eliminates toxic discharge neutralizing costs and pipeline corrosion.

Conserves Process Water via Reduced Blowdown

Permits higher cycles of concentration (CoC) in cooling towers and boilers, conserving millions of liters of process water annually.
